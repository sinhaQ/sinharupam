MedCompare

A price comparison site for medicines across Indian online pharmacies — PharmEasy, 1mg, Netmeds, Apollo, MedPlus and others. It has a three-step phone and OTP sign-in, pincode-based delivery zone detection that shows a realistic delivery date per pharmacy, ratings with review breakdowns, and filter and sort controls.

I also wrote the Express backend behind it: MongoDB models, JWT authentication, OTP flows, and medicine search and comparison APIs.

Started as one HTML file, then refactored into a proper HTML/CSS/JS structure as it grew.
